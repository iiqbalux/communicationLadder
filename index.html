Our Ethos

Expert therapy, without the wait

At Communication Ladder we are passionate about helping children develop their confidence and ability to communicate.

We work closely with families and education settings across Greater Manchester to provide bespoke support to children and young people.

We believe communication is key in empowering children, young people and their families.

Our Approach

Therapy that works with children

Every child is different. Our sessions adapt to your child's interests and attention, while giving parents the tools to carry progress home.

Play-Based Sessions

Most of our interventions are carried out through play to maintain children's attention and make learning feel natural and fun-not like hard work.

Parents at the Heart

We actively invite parents into sessions. You'll receive strategies and resources every week so you can support your child's progress at home between appointments.

Child-Led Where Possible

Where appropriate, we follow the child's interests and lead. Children learn best when they feel in control-our sessions adapt to what motivates them most.

Home Visits Available

Therapy and assessment sessions can be carried out in your child's home. A quiet area is required for assessment. Online sessions are also available where appropriate.

School Collaboration

We work closely with schools, sharing intervention plans and strategies so your child receives consistent support both at home and in the classroom.

Skip the 2-Year Wait

We understand NHS wait times can stretch to 2 years. We bridge that gap-providing an initial therapy plan so your child can get started immediately.
